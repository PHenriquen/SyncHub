# SyncHub Brand Identity

## Core Direction
- Concept: unified flow between channels, tasks and automation.
- Personality: operational, intelligent, focused.
- Visual metaphor: connected orbit (signal routing and synchronization).

## Logo System
- Mark (icon only): `src/web/assets/logo-mark.svg`

Usage:
- Use "SYNC + mark + HUB" lockup for login/header and presentation.
- Use the mark for compact surfaces (sidebar, app icon, badges).
- Keep clear space around logo equal to the icon dot diameter.

## Color Palette
- `#2f4a72` primary background
- `#233a5e` deep background
- `#3a5f95` primary brand
- `#2a4672` primary dark
- `#84aef4` accent
- `#ffffff` neutral light
- `#182a45` neutral text

## Typography
- Primary: `Space Grotesk`
- Mono/support: `IBM Plex Mono`

## UI Principles
- Dark control surfaces + bright operational workspace.
- High contrast for critical information and priorities.
- Minimal iconography and direct labels.
- Motion and effects should be subtle and purposeful.
